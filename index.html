About Us

Stuttgart GAA is a community club promoting Gaelic games, including Gaelic football and hurling, in the Stuttgart region. Active since 2016, we are open to all skill levels and nationalities, we combine sport, teamwork, and cultural exchange, connecting Irish expatriates with the wider international community while growing Gaelic sports in Germany.

Training

  • Training Schedule

    We hold 2-hour training sessions on Sunday mornings/afternoons from March to October. Each week alternates between Gaelic football and hurling, giving players a chance to experience both sports.

  • Who Can Join

    Everyone is welcome, from complete beginners to experienced players. Our sessions are designed to be inclusive and fun for all skill levels.

  • Location

    We currently train in Waldau, stuttgart (Beside die Waldauerin resturant). Join our WhatsApp Channels for the most up-to date information regarding training sessions & social events.

Tournaments & Events

We regularly compete in local and regional tournaments throughout the season, traveling to cities like Munich, Zurich, Bern, Frankfurt, Strasbourg, and Berlin.

We also team up with other clubs in the area to mix things up, playing International Rules, American Football, and more.

Throughout the summer, we host a range of social events — including quiz nights, BBQs, international soccer and rugby games — often in collaboration with local Irish pubs.

Become a Member

If you’re interested in joining the club, follow our social media & come along to a training session on Sunday morning to try it out!

Contact us via our social media channels for information regarding membership, training and other queries.

If you’d like to become a member, registration is available through Fioreann.ie
